DESCRIPTION

A classic floral-aldehyde fragrance known for its complex, powdery, and abstract scent profile. Its key tones include a sparkling, bright top note of aldehydes combined with citrus (bergamot, neroli), a heart of floral luxury (May rose, jasmine, ylang-ylang), and a warm, woody base (sandalwood, vanilla, musk).
